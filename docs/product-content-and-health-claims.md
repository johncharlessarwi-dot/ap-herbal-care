# Product information and health-claims review guide

This guide supports accurate, responsible product pages and customer communications for AP Herbal Care.

## Product facts
- Confirm product name, ingredients, pack size, usage directions, warnings, storage, and supplier details against an approved source.
- Keep product availability and pricing current; show when information was last reviewed.
- Do not publish unsupported claims about treating, curing, or preventing disease.
- Do not imply that a product replaces professional diagnosis or prescribed treatment.

## Content review
1. Draft plain-language information from verified product documentation.
2. Separate factual ingredients and directions from marketing language.
3. Have a designated reviewer check health-related wording and warnings.
4. Remove claims that cannot be substantiated or legally reviewed.
5. Correct inaccurate content promptly and retain a change record.

## Customer questions
Provide general product information only. For symptoms, pregnancy, chronic conditions, medication interactions, or adverse reactions, encourage the customer to consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Incident escalation
Record the product, batch or lot when available, concern, date, and action taken. Escalate suspected adverse events to the responsible supplier and appropriate regulator according to applicable requirements. Do not publish private customer details.
